Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about joining, rules, ranks, and support.

Getting Started

How do I join DonutSMP?
Open Minecraft → Multiplayer → Add Server → enter donutsmp.net with port 19132. DonutSMP supports both Java and Bedrock editions.
What version of Minecraft does DonutSMP run?
DonutSMP runs on modern Minecraft versions and is compatible with most clients. Check the official Discord for the exact current version.
What is DonutSMP known for?
DonutSMP is known for base-hunting, Crystal PvP, a player-driven economy with auction house and orders, and six global proxies covering NA East, NA West, EU Central, EU West, Asia, and Oceania.

Rules & Gameplay

What are the main rules on DonutSMP?
The main rules ban hacked clients, movement mods, ESP, freecam, auto-clickers, macros, item duplication, IRL trading, cross-server trading, staff impersonation, and using more than 5 accounts. See the full rules page for details.
How do I report a player or bug?
Report bugs, glitches, and rule-breakers to DonutSMP staff through the official Discord or by opening a ticket at support.donutsmp.net.
Can I use mods on DonutSMP?
Only mods that don't provide a gameplay advantage. Cosmetic mods, shaders, and performance mods (OptiFine, Sodium, etc.) are generally fine. Anything that changes movement, visibility, combat, or automation is banned.

Store & Ranks

What's the difference between Donut+, Donut++, and Donut+++?
Donut+ — 9 homes, 45 auction/order slots ($4.99/mo).
Donut++ — 27 homes, 90 auction/order slots ($9.99/mo).
Donut+++ — 90 homes, 90 auction/order slots ($14.99/mo).
See the store page for direct checkout links.
What are Shards?
Shards are the secondary currency on DonutSMP, separate from money. They can be used in the Shard Shop to buy exclusive items. Packages range from 400 shards ($0.99) to 70,000 shards ($99.99), with bonus percentages on larger tiers.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by DonutSMP support, not by this site. Open a ticket at support.donutsmp.net with your transaction details.
Does this site process my payment?
No. All "Buy" buttons link directly to the official DonutSMP store at store.donutsmp.net. We do not handle payments, store card details, or access any transaction data.

Community & This Site

Does DonutSMP have a Discord?
Yes. DonutSMP has one of the largest Minecraft Discord communities with over 1 million members. Join at discord.com/invite/donutsmp.
Is DonutSMP Hub the official site?
No. DonutSMP Hub is an unofficial, fan-made community companion site. It is not affiliated with DonutSMP, DrDonutt, Mojang, or Microsoft. See Terms & Policies for the full disclaimer.
Where do the player counts come from?
Live player counts come from mcstatus.io, a public Minecraft server status API. Discord member counts come from Discord's public invite API. Both are fetched server-side at build time and refreshed hourly, so the numbers you see are at most 1 hour old.
