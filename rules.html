Minecraft Server Rules

Community-maintained reference. Always check the official DonutSMP Discord for the latest rules.

No Hacked Clients
Using hacked clients like Meteor, Wurst, Impact, Future, or any client that provides unfair advantages (fly, killaura, x-ray, reach, etc.) is strictly forbidden.
No Movement Mods
No mods that change how you move: speed, flight, jesus, no-fall, bunny-hop, or anything that alters vanilla movement.
No Inventory Mod
No mods that let you view, sort, or manipulate other players' inventories, chests, or storage you can't normally access.
No Health Indicators
Showing other players' health values in PvP gives an unfair information advantage. Not allowed.
No Radar
No radar, minimap, or entity tracking that reveals players or mobs outside your vanilla render distance.
No Freecam
Detaching the camera from your body to scout, spy, or explore is not allowed.
No ESP
No seeing players, mobs, chests, or ores through walls (player ESP, chest ESP, storage ESP, cave finder, etc.).
No Auto or Easy Place
Auto-place, scaffolding, and easy-place mods bypass normal building mechanics and are banned.
No Macros or Scripts
No auto-running command sequences, chat scripts, or macro chains that perform actions for you.
No Auto Clicker
Any software or hardware that clicks for you (auto-clickers, turbo buttons, CPS tools) is not allowed.
No Mouse Tweaks / Scrollers
Mods like Mouse Tweaks, Inventory Tweaks, or scroll-to-craft that let you move items faster than vanilla.
No Crafting Modifications
No mods that change crafting behavior: auto-craft, recipe book exploitation, or altered recipe outputs.
No Abusing Bugs
Don't exploit unintended mechanics. Even if it works, using a bug to gain items, power, or an advantage is punishable.
No Attempted Duplicating
Attempting to duplicate items carries the same punishment as actually duplicating them.
No Duplicating Items
Any form of item duplication is a permanent-ban offense.
No IRL Trading
Selling, buying, or trading in-game items, currency, or accounts for real money is forbidden.
No Invite Rewards
Rewarding players for inviting others (in-game items, cash, ranks) is not allowed.
No External Gambling
No outside gambling sites, coin-flip bots, or wagering systems involving in-game or real value.
No Discord Boost Rewards
Giving in-game rewards in exchange for Discord boosts is not permitted.
No Cross-Server Trading
Trading DonutSMP items for items, currency, or accounts on other servers is banned.
No Staff Impersonation
Pretending to be staff through names, nicknames, ranks, or messages is a serious offense.
No Using More Than 5 Accounts
Each player may use up to 5 accounts. Using alts to bypass bans, farm rewards, or abuse mechanics is not allowed.
No Finding or Using the Seed
Reverse-engineering the world seed or using external tools to locate structures, ores, or terrain is banned.
No Spamming Voice Chat
No soundboards, screaming, earrape, music spam, or anything that ruins voice chat for others.
Report all Bugs, Glitches, and Cheaters
Help us keep the server fair. Report any bugs, glitches, or rule-breakers to staff instead of abusing them.
